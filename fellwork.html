The Program

Three sessions a week, periodised around your running. Heavy work drives durability and running economy; the upper day is where the mirror progress lives; Saturday builds the race.

Weekly rhythm

TueLower A — Heavy · stacked with a harder run day so easy days stay easy. Run first if doubling; lift PM.
ThuUpper — Build · near-zero cost to your legs. Safe even in peak weeks.
SatLower B + Core · race-specific, sub-maximal. Must not dent Sunday's long run — if it does, cut volume, not the session.
SunLong run (2–4 h). No lifting within 24 h before.
Race-proximity dials

Normal weeks: all three sessions.
Big running weeks / feeling flat: drop Sat first, then trim Tue to 2 heavy sets per lift. Never grind.
Final 3 weeks pre A-race: Tue → short maintenance (2×3–5 crisp), Thu → light, Sat → off.
Week after an A-race: Upper only, then rebuild over 2 weeks.

Rules that protect the running (read once, believe forever)

1. Stop every heavy set with 1–2 clean reps in the tank. Strength grows anyway; your Achilles thanks you.
2. Heavy = few reps, long rests (2–3 min). It should feel crisp, not crushing.
3. Soreness that changes your running gait means you overdid it — cut 30% next time.
4. Ankle or toe flare-up: swap impact and deep-flexion work for isometrics and carries. Never train through a gout flare.
5. The running always wins. A skipped lift costs nothing; a compromised long run costs a week.

Fuel

The gout framework

Your suspected urate sensitivity shapes the plan more than the training does. Good news: the evidence points to a veg-forward, dairy-friendly, Asian-leaning diet being close to ideal.

Green — eat freely
All vegetables (yes, even the high-purine ones — plant purines don't raise gout risk) · tofu, tempeh, edamame · lentils, chickpeas, beans · eggs · low-fat dairy, Greek yogurt, skyr, paneer (protective — aim for a serving daily) · whole grains, rice, noodles, oats · all fruit, especially cherries & berries · nuts & seeds · coffee & tea · chilli, ginger, garlic, all spices
Amber — moderate portions
Salmon, trout, white fish (2–3 palm-size portions/week is fine) · chicken (your 1–2×/week cap fits perfectly) · fish sauce, oyster sauce, dashi, dried shrimp — use as seasoning, teaspoons not tablespoons · wine (unlike beer, doesn't raise urate — still keep it modest) · fruit juice & honey (fructose raises urate)
Red — avoid
Organ meats · anchovies, sardines, mackerel, herring · mussels, scallops, and go easy on prawns/squid · red meat & processed meat · beer (including alcohol-free with yeast extract) · yeast extract (Marmite) · sugary drinks & excess added sugar · crash diets and dehydration — both spike urate, so the weight loss below stays gentle

During any flare: hydrate hard, skip alcohol entirely, keep training below pain, and see your GP about a urate blood test — confirming your baseline beats guessing.
